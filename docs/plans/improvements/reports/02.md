## Outside scope

- `packages/ui-cli/src/checks/library.ts`: two identifiers (`mod.DropDown`,
  `mod.MenuDropDown`) had to follow R9; without them `library:check` fails on an
  undefined export. The Ownership section lists only `library-artifact.ts`.
- `packages/pagopa-ui/.oxlintrc.json`: `controlComponents` had to follow R11,
  otherwise the `label-has-associated-control` rule silently stops recognising
  `TextareaField` as a labelled control.
- `packages/tokens/src/foundation-exceptions.json` keys
  `src/components/data-display/microevento.tsx`. `packages/tokens/**` is
  forbidden, so R5 keeps that module path (`microevento.tsx` exporting
  `MicroEvent`) instead of renaming the file; `foundation:check` stays green.
- `docs/reports/**` and `docs/design-requests/**` still name the old exports.
  They are historical records outside the Ownership section; the plan lets only
  `docs/decisions/**`, `docs/plans/**` and the released CHANGELOG sections keep
  the old names.
- The R10 type exports `FigmaIconName` and `FigmaPictogramName` keep their names:
  the row lists only the two runtime lists, and the types are generated in
  `packages/artwork`, which is forbidden.
- `apps/storybook/src/UIProvider.stories.tsx` is titled
  `Components/Composites/UIProvider` instead of the step's
  `Components/UIProvider`: the story taxonomy test accepts only
  `Components/<section>/<Component>`, and its test file is outside the
  Ownership section.
- `src/components/templates/figma/TemplateShell.tsx` keeps its two plain
  anchors: both are in-page `#main-content` links, the case step 6 excludes.
- `registry.json` keeps `namesExport: "figmaIconNames"`/`"figmaPictogramNames"`
  and the two `Icons` story calls keep those keys: they name constants in
  `packages/artwork`, which is forbidden.
