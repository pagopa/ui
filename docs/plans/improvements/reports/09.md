## Open risks

- The three new component types (Content display, State and progress, Module)
  are a design-system taxonomy decision made to keep the map honest: 51 of the
  107 exports had no fitting row. A product annex built from the table inherits
  their criteria.
- `target-size` is now enforced by the tag set and is easy to trip in a new
  story; the fix belongs to the story or the component that introduces it.
- `ErrorState` still has no story that triggers its copy announcement, so its
  `live-region` behaviour is proven only by the markup check.

### Tagged `live-region` stories

- `Overlay` → `Playground` (the status region carries the wait label).
- `Banner` → `SourceCase` (the banner's status region carries the message).
- `SnackbarErrorCode` → `ClipboardSuccess` (the play copies the code, the
  `output` announces it).
- `TableList` → `Empty` (the empty state's status region carries the sentence).
- `FormControls` → `WithErrorSummary` (the play submits, the summary announces).

Without a story: `ErrorState` announces the copy outcome of its error code, but
no story clicks the copy control, so no story can prove it. Per the action, no
story was added.

### Exports mapped to `null`

- `SafeArea`: layout wrapper — it centres its children and renders no UI of its
  own.
- `Spacer`: paints nothing and is hidden from assistive technology.
- `UIProvider`: renders no UI — it supplies the router link and locale contexts.
