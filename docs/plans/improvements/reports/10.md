## Open risks

- The per-module build emits the bundled tokens modules under `dist/tokens/src/`;
  `tokens.js` imports them relative. They ship in the tarball (30 KB) and the
  path is asserted only indirectly, by the consumer import. A future rename of
  that folder changes a published deep path, not the documented entries.
- `prepare-types` now inlines two packages; a third bundled package needs a row
  in its table.
- The typed list reports `cssName: "text-*"` for typography entries, while
  `tokens.css` declares only `--figma-*`; the contract file keeps the two
  projections apart with the `js:` prefix.
