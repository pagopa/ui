## What changed

- `packages/ui-cli/src/checks/foundation.ts`: three rules — `default-breakpoint`,
  `mobile-text-class`, `default-container-query`; the first rejects Tailwind's
  viewport variants (`sm:`, `md:`, `lg:`, `xl:`, `2xl:` and their `max-` forms),
  the other two freeze the off-token responsive patterns.
- `packages/ui-cli/tests/checks/foundation.test.mjs`: one hit and one miss per
  rule; the breakpoint misses cover `tablet:flex`, `@md/alert:flex`,
  `data-[state=open]:` and `max-w-screen-sm`.
- `packages/tokens/src/foundation-baseline.json`: the three counts, and the
  `default-breakpoint` ceiling lowered to 0 after the migration.
- `packages/ui-cli/src/figma/tokens.ts`: `emitCss` writes
  `--breakpoint-*: initial;` as the first line of the breakpoint block.
- `packages/tokens/src/figma.generated.css`: the same line, by hand, in the same
  position; `tokens:check` proves the file still matches the generator.
- `packages/ui-cli/tests/figma/tokens.test.mjs`: the emitted CSS resets the
  Tailwind scale before the first Figma breakpoint.
- 24 component files and 5 story files: every `sm:`/`md:`/`lg:`/`xl:`/`2xl:` and
  `max-*` variant mapped to `tablet:`/`tablet-md:`/`desktop:`/`desktop-xl:`/
  `desktop-xxl:`; one comment that named `md:gap-*` follows the new name.
- `.agents/skills/figma-to-code/references/devices.md`: the viewport scope names
  the five Figma variants with their widths and says the theme resets Tailwind's
  scale and `default-breakpoint` rejects it; the two class examples and the
  tablet trap use `tablet:` instead of `sm`/`md`.
- `packages/pagopa-ui/CHANGELOG.md`, `.nx/version-plans/version-plan-1792878870021.md`:
  the `### Changed` entry and the `minor` plan.
