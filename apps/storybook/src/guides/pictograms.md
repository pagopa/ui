`PagoPAPictogram` renders the published pictograms as light and dark artwork,
scaled by `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg` or a number) with an optional visible caption.

```tsx
<PagoPAPictogram name="Access" size="md" />
<PagoPAPictogram name="Access" background="dark" label="Accesso" />
```

`background="dark"` selects the artwork drawn for a dark surface, not a dark
application theme, which stays light-only.

## Provenance and licence

The pictograms are the Flaticon "Blue" collection by **SeyfDesigner**, the source
linked from the Figma "• Pictograms 🟢" page
(`flaticon.com/authors/seyfdesigner/blue`). Light and dark are the two source
backgrounds. The attribution terms of the acquisition licence still need
confirmation: keep the credit `SeyfDesigner — Flaticon` with the collection link
